About acro yoga

What to expect

In acro yoga, a base and a flyer work together through partner poses, lifts and balances. Classes focus on communication, safe technique and confidence, and suit beginners as well as more experienced practitioners.

Past students have described the classes as fun, well explained and a good balance of challenge and calm.
