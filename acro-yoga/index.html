Acro Yoga in Bristol

Acro yoga is partner acrobatics combined with yoga. It builds balance, trust and body awareness, and it is a lot of fun.

About acro yoga

What to expect

In acro yoga, a base and a flyer work together through partner poses, lifts and balances. Classes focus on communication, safe technique and building confidence, and they suit beginners as well as more experienced practitioners.

Past students have described the classes as fun, well explained and a good balance of challenge and calm.
