Acro yoga in Bristol

Ever wanted to fly? Learn the foundations of partner-based acro yoga in a safe, playful and welcoming class.

Status: classes are currently paused. Follow Acroyoga with Jan on Facebook for updates.

What is acro yoga?

Acroyoga combines yoga, Thai massage and acrobatics. In partners and small groups you take turns as base, flyer and spotter, building trust, balance and communication while having a lot of fun.

  • No yoga or acrobatics experience needed
  • No partner needed
  • All levels, from beginners to intermediates
  • Suitable for every body shape and size

What a class looks like

Each 90-minute open-level class includes partner calibration exercises, acrobatics, acroyoga and Thai massage elements. You’ll learn core concepts, foundations, fun moves and transitions, and it can be a bit of a workout too. Expect to laugh, be a little nervous and meet new people.

When classes run

Classes previously ran on Tuesdays from 6:15pm to 7:45pm at The Exercise Club in Clifton, Bristol. Drop-ins were £14, monthly membership £50, and MoveGB was accepted. They are currently on pause, so please check Facebook for updates before travelling.

Your teacher

Jan Tabaka has taught acroyoga in Bristol for around six years, with guest teachers supporting. He is also a qualified personal trainer, a certified Thai yoga masseur and the founder of Bristol Bachata.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need yoga or acrobatics experience?

No. The class is for anyone who’s keen to try it, and you learn the basics from the start.

Do I need a partner?

No. Most people come alone. We rotate partners and work in groups of three, so you’ll always have someone to work with.

Do I need to be strong or flexible?

No. Technique matters more than strength, and size doesn’t decide who can fly or base. Acroyoga is for every body.

Are classes running at the moment?

Classes are currently paused due to low interest. Follow the Acroyoga with Jan Facebook page for news and any restart dates.

How much did classes cost?
