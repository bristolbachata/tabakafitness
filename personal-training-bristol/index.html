Frequently asked questions

How much does personal training cost in Bristol?

A one-hour session costs £65. Packages are discussed at your first session, so you can choose the option that suits your goals.

Do you offer small group training?

Yes. As well as one-to-one sessions, I offer semi-private sessions for groups of 2 to 4 people.

Can I combine personal training with nutrition support?

Yes. Many clients add nutrition guidance, and some start with a body composition and nutritional analysis. Coaching is also available online.
