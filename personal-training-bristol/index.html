Personal Trainer in Bristol

Personal training is about motivation, getting results and getting them the way you want. Sessions are one-to-one or small group, in a well-equipped gym close to Bristol city centre.

Book a session See prices

Approach

A programme built around you

Every client is different. I start with your goals, your current fitness and any injuries or limitations, then design a bespoke health and fitness programme that you can follow safely and enjoy.

Training is backed by sport science. I use it to choose exercises, set progressions and adjust the plan as you get stronger.

Goals

What personal training can help with

Tone up and get lean

Build muscle definition while reducing body fat, with nutrition guidance alongside training.

Weight loss or muscle gain

Plans for losing weight or building muscle, based on where you are starting from.

Improve functional strength

Strength you can use in everyday life, at work and in other sports.

Improve sports performance

Training to help you perform better in your chosen sport.

Mobility and pain relief

Exercises to improve flexibility and mobility and help ease pain and stiffness.

Nutrition and overall health

Practical nutrition support, combined with body composition tracking. Learn more.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does personal training cost in Bristol?

A one-hour session costs £60, and £55 per session after that. Packages are available on request, with monthly instalments or upfront payment.

Do you offer small group training?

Yes. As well as one-to-one sessions, I offer semi-private sessions for groups of 2 to 4 people.

Can I combine personal training with nutrition support?

Yes. Many clients add nutrition guidance, and some start with a body composition and nutritional analysis. You can also do coaching online.
