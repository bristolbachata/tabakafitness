Frequently asked questions

How much does a personal training session cost?

A one-hour one-to-one session is £65. Small-group sessions cost more and are confirmed with Jan before booking. Packages are tailored to you at your consultation, and monthly instalments or upfront payments are both available.

Where do sessions take place?

Sessions run at LA Gym, 20 Easton Rd, Bristol BS5 0BY, a spacious, well-equipped gym close to Bristol city centre. Online sessions are also available.

I’m a complete beginner. Is personal training right for me?

Yes. Many clients start with little or no gym experience. Your programme begins at your level, and I coach technique so you feel confident and stay injury-free.

Can you help with weight loss and muscle gain?

Yes. Programmes combine training with practical nutrition guidance for fat loss, muscle gain, strength, sports performance, posture or general health.

What happens in the first session?

Start with the initial consultation: a structured 60-minute session covering your goals, medical and exercise history, body composition and nutritional analysis. You leave with a complete plan tailored to you.
