Why online coaching

More than a plan on a page

There is plenty of training and nutrition information online. What many people need is someone who helps them put it into practice and stick with it. That is what online coaching is for.

You get a training programme, nutrition guidance and a coach who checks progress with you, so the plan changes as your life and results change.

What is included

How online coaching works

Personalised training

A programme built around your goals, experience and the equipment you have, whether that is a full gym or home kit.

Nutrition coaching

A review of your current eating habits to find the limiting factors and the changes that will help most with your body composition and training goals.

Video sessions

Live video calls for coaching, form checks and planning, with your trainer working alongside you.

Body composition analysis

A 60-minute online body composition and nutrition analysis is available. It covers body fat percentage, lean mass and body type. Read more.

Who it is for

Online coaching suits you if

  • You want to train at home or at your own gym
  • Your schedule or location makes in-person sessions hard
  • You want to improve your nutrition as well as your training
  • You struggle with motivation and want regular accountability

Frequently asked questions

How does online personal training work?

Sessions take place by video call. Your training programme is designed around your goals, and nutrition coaching looks at your current eating habits and what should change.

Do I need to be in Bristol?

No. Online coaching is available to clients wherever they live, so you can train from home or your own gym.
