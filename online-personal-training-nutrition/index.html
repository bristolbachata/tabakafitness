Frequently asked questions

Do I need gym equipment?

No. Programmes can be designed with or without equipment, for the gym, your living room or the garden.

Who is online training suitable for?

Any age, fitness level, goal or health condition. You can train alone, with a partner or even with your children.

How do sessions work?

One-hour sessions run by video call, and your programme and nutrition plan are tailored to you. I keep you accountable and track progress between sessions.
