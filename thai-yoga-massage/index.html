Thai Yoga Massage in Bristol

Thai yoga massage combines gentle stretching, rhythmic pressure and assisted movement. It is a good fit if you carry tension, want more mobility or simply need to recover.

Ask about availability

About the treatment

What to expect

Thai yoga massage is a floor-based treatment. You stay clothed and comfortable while your practitioner guides you through stretches and applies pressure along the body’s lines of energy.

Because it combines massage with movement, many people find it helps with stiffness, tight muscles and general flexibility. Jan is also an experienced yoga and movement practitioner, so the session can be tailored to how your body moves.

Before you book: tell Jan about any injuries, medical conditions, pregnancy or recent surgery so the session can be adapted safely. If you have a medical concern, speak to your GP first.
