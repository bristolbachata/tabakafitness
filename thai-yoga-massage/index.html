About the treatment

What to expect

Thai yoga massage combines gentle stretching, rhythmic pressure and assisted movement. Many people find it helps with stiffness, tight muscles and flexibility.

Jan is an experienced yoga and movement practitioner, so the session can be shaped around how your body moves.

Sessions are available on request. Get in touch to check availability.

Before you book

Tell Jan about

  • Pregnancy or recent surgery
  • What you would like to get from the session
