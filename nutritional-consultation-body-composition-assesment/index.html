Understanding the numbers

What is body composition?

Body composition is the proportion of fat to lean muscle tissue in your body, usually expressed as a percentage of your total weight.

Body weight on its own is not a clear indicator of health. It does not show how much of your weight is fat and how much is lean mass. Tracking body composition over time shows whether training and nutrition are working, even when the scale barely moves.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is body composition?

It is the proportion of fat to lean muscle tissue in your body. Body weight alone does not show how much of it is fat and how much is lean mass.

Do I need to keep a food diary?

Yes. You are asked to keep a food diary for 5 to 7 days. It is used for the nutritional analysis and your report.

Can I do this if I am not training with you?

Yes. The assessment and nutritional consultation are available on their own, and many clients use them as a starting point before deciding on training.

Is a body composition result a medical diagnosis?

No. Results are a guide to help you track changes and plan nutrition and training. If you have a medical concern, speak to your GP.
