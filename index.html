Bristol Bachata

Bachata classes and parties

I founded Bristol Bachata in 2018, and it has grown into one of the UK’s biggest bachata schools. Our group classes are the easiest way to start: you meet friendly people, learn with a supportive crowd and gain confidence fast, whatever your level. No partner needed, and the first class is free for absolute beginners.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What happens in the initial consultation?

It is a structured 60-minute session, not just a chat. I review your goals, medical and exercise history and how you like to train, carry out a body composition and nutritional analysis, and build a complete plan tailored to you.

Where do the sessions take place?

One-to-one and small-group sessions take place at LA Gym, 20 Easton Rd, Bristol BS5 0BY, close to Bristol city centre. Online coaching works from anywhere, and bachata classes run at Totos By The River on Redcliff Street.

How much does personal training in Bristol cost?

An individual one-hour personal training session is £65. Packages are tailored to you and priced at your consultation, with monthly instalments or upfront payment available. Online coaching starts from £39 a month.

Do you offer online personal training?

Yes. Online training and nutrition coaching suit any fitness level and can be done with or without equipment, at home or outdoors, by video call or through a coaching app.

Is personal training suitable for beginners, injuries or health conditions?

Yes. Your plan is built around your medical and exercise history, so exercises are adapted to your level and any injuries or conditions. If a doctor or physio has given you advice, share it and I will work around it.

Is Tabaka Fitness related to Tabata workouts?

Ha, I get this one a lot! My name may look similar, but I am Tabaka, not Tabata, and I am not the Japanese scientist behind the famous 20-seconds-on, 10-seconds-off protocol. Tabaka Fitness is not connected with Tabata workouts, apps or brands. I am all about tailoring the approach to each client rather than a one-size-fits-all stopwatch routine. :)
