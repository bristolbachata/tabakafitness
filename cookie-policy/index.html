Cookie policy

Which cookies and similar storage this website uses, why, and how to change your choices at any time.

Last updated 24 October 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. We also use a similar technology called local storage. They help a site work, remember your choices and, if you allow it, measure how it is used.

What we use

Cookies and similar storage on tabakafitness.com
NameWhat it doesTypeHow long
tabaka_consentRemembers your cookie choice so we do not ask you again.Strictly necessary (browser local storage)12 months
_ga, _ga_*Google Analytics: tells us anonymously how many people visit and which pages are useful.Analytics (only if you accept)Up to 2 years

Analytics cookies are never set unless you choose “Accept all” or switch analytics on. We do not use advertising or marketing cookies.

Your choices

When you first visit, you can accept all, reject non-essential, or customise your choices. You can change your mind at any time with the button below or the “Cookie settings” link in the footer. Switching analytics off also removes the analytics cookies we have set.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, although some parts of a website may then not work as expected.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to other services such as Facebook, YouTube and our booking and payment pages. These sites set their own cookies and have their own policies, which we do not control.

More information

For how we handle personal information more generally, see our privacy policy. Questions? Email jantabaka@gmail.com.
