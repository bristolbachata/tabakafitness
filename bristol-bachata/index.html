Bristol Bachata

Founded by Jan Tabaka in 2018, Bristol Bachata runs weekly sensual bachata classes and social dancing. Bookings, passes and tickets are on its own website.

Visit Bristol Bachata

Classes

What to expect

  • Weekly classes on Mondays and Wednesdays
  • Classes for beginners, improvers and more advanced dancers
  • Lead and follow technique, musicality and styling
  • Social dancing after the classes
  • No partner needed, just turn up and dance

Current times and venues are listed on the Bristol Bachata website, so check there before you come.

About Bristol Bachata

One of the UK’s friendliest bachata schools

Bristol Bachata has been featured on BBC One and BBC Four. Jan has more than 13 years of sensual bachata experience, gained through masterclasses with Korke and Judith, the creators of sensual bachata.

Bristol Bachata is run separately from Tabaka Fitness personal training. For training enquiries, contact Jan here.
