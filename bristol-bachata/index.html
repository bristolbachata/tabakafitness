Bristol Bachata

Bristol Bachata is my dance school, founded in 2018. It runs weekly sensual bachata classes and social dancing in the city, and it has its own website for bookings and tickets.

Visit Bristol Bachata

Classes

What to expect

  • Weekly classes on Mondays and Wednesdays
  • Classes for beginners, improvers and more advanced dancers
  • Sensual bachata, with lead and follow technique, musicality and styling
  • Social dancing after the classes
  • No partner needed. Just turn up and dance

Classes are taught by Jan Tabaka with guest and resident teachers. Current times and venues are listed on the Bristol Bachata website, so check there before you come.

Booking

Tickets, passes and questions

Drop-in passes, monthly memberships, private classes and party tickets are all available from the Bristol Bachata booking store. Questions about the classes can be asked through the same website.
