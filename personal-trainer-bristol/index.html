Meet Jan Tabaka

Certified personal trainer, body composition specialist and founder of Bristol Bachata.

Background

Certified, science-led

My name is Jan Tabaka. I am a certified personal trainer with a BSc in Sports and Exercise Science and more than 12 years of experience in the fitness industry.

I build a bespoke health and fitness programme for every client, designed to reach your goals safely and effectively, and to fit the life you actually lead.

  • Certified personal trainer
  • BSc Sports and Exercise Science
  • 12+ years of coaching experience
  • Body composition analysis and nutrition guidance
  • Founder of Bristol Bachata, 2018

Beyond the gym

Training, movement and dance

I also teach acro yoga and Thai yoga massage. Since 2018 I have run Bristol Bachata, a dance school known for its inclusive, friendly community. I have trained with Korke and Judith, the creators of sensual bachata, and many other teachers.
