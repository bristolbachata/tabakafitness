About Jan Tabaka

Certified personal trainer, nutrition-minded coach and founder of Bristol Bachata.

Background

My name is Jan Tabaka. I am a certified personal trainer with a degree in sports and exercise science and more than 12 years of experience in the fitness industry.

I recognise that every client is an individual, so I build a bespoke health and fitness programme for each person. That programme is designed to reach your goals safely and effectively, and it fits the life you actually lead.

Alongside training, I offer body composition analysis and nutrition guidance, so you can see what is changing and why.

Beyond the gym

I also teach acrobatics and Thai yoga massage. Since 2018 I have run Bristol Bachata, a dance school in Bristol that is known for its inclusive, friendly community. I have trained with Korke and Judith, the creators of sensual bachata, and many other teachers around the world.

You can find the dance school at Bristol Bachata. Acro yoga is also part of my work, although the classes are not running at the moment.

What I focus on

  • Personal training that is safe, progressive and tailored
  • Nutrition guidance based on how you actually eat
  • Body composition tracking so progress is measured, not guessed
  • Online coaching for clients who train from home or travel
